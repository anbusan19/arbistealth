ArbiStealth

Private trade execution with verifiable agent accountability, built on Arbitrum.

01 · Problem

On-chain trading is transparent by default. That's now a liability.

Every order is a public target

Size, direction, and wallet are visible before and after execution, inviting front-running and copy-trading.

Agents trade with no accountability

Autonomous agents are starting to act on users' behalf, with no verifiable record of whether they acted honestly.

Agent payments have no settlement standard

Agent-to-agent and agent-to-protocol fees settle ad hoc, with no receipt trail tied to the agent that earned them.

Privacy and identity live apart

Stealth-address tools and agent-identity tools exist separately. Nothing unifies them into one execution path.

02 · Solution

Three primitives, one protocol.

ArbiStealth combines privacy, agent identity, and accountable payments into a single trade execution path.

Privacy

Stealth settlement

ERC-5564 stealth addresses and the ERC-6538 registry hide trade destination and history from outside observers.

Agent identity

On-chain reputation

ERC-8004 gives every trading agent a persistent identity, so its track record is verifiable, not claimed.

Payments

Accountable fees

x402 settles agent routing fees, with each receipt attached directly to that agent's on-chain reputation.

03 · How it works

One trade, start to finish.

01

Generate a stealth meta-address

The user, or their agent, derives a stealth meta-address before any trade is placed.

02

Agent submits a signed intent

The trade intent is signed and tied to the agent's ERC-8004 identity.

03

Intent is matched and routed

The Settlement Router matches the intent and routes it for execution.

04

Trade settles to the stealth address

Settlement is invisible on-chain to outside observers.

05

Routing fee settles via x402

The fee receipt is written directly to the agent's on-chain reputation record.

06

Reputation compounds

Over repeated trades, honest agents become verifiably trustworthy, not just self-described as such.

04 · Why this, why now

Agents are moving from experiment to production. Nobody can verify them yet.

Agent identity is brand-new

ERC-8004 is a new standard. Almost nothing combines agent identity with transaction privacy today.

Agent-native payments just arrived

x402 gives agents a settlement rail of their own, built for machine-to-machine fees, not retrofitted from human checkout flows.

Arbitrum fits the cost profile

Cheap enough to support frequent agent-driven transactions, mature enough to carry real liquidity.

The gap is structural, not incremental

Privacy tools and reputation tools are each maturing on their own. Nobody has wired them into the same settlement path.

07 · How we're different

Privacy and accountability, not privacy or accountability.

Fluidkey

Stealth addresses, live on Arbitrum. No agent layer, no payment accountability tied to it.

Unwallet

Privacy-focused wallet infrastructure. Not built around autonomous agents or reputation.

CanHav Research

Combines agent identity with privacy using FHE, a heavier cryptographic approach.

ArbiStealth

Stealth settlement plus x402-backed agent reputation: a lighter, more composable path to the same guarantee, with payment receipts written directly into the agent's track record.
